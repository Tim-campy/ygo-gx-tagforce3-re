## C1. Calendar block: pass

The 9 words at `S + 0x1a24c` (`0x08abfa08`):

| Moment | `+00` t | `+04` slot | `+08` | `+0c` day no. | `+10` day | `+14` light | `+18` | `+1c` weekday | `+20` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 2 | 2 | 5 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| After leaving a map | 3 | 3 | 5 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| After "End the day" | 8 | 1 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 1 |
| Day shown as "Day 8, Monday" | 50 | 1 | 5 | 8 | 7 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |

- Every value follows the formulas in `world.md`. "End the day" moves `t` to the first slot of the next day (3 → 8), so a day is 7 slots.
- The day-6 reading (shown as Sunday) had `+0x1c = 0`, and day 7 is shown as Monday with `+0x1c = 1`. **Weekday 0 = Sunday, 1 = Monday**: the game starts on a Monday, and the day off of `world_is_day_off` (day index 6 of each week) is Sunday.
- Writers: the slot counter is written at `rel_story 0x08eaacd8` (leaving a map, inside `world_advance_time_slot`) and `0x08eab0e0` (End the day, `world_advance_to_next_day`). `world_update_calendar` then writes `+0x04` (four times, `0x08eac288`–`0x08eac2a4`), `+0x10`, `+0x0c`, `+0x14`, `+0x1c` (`0x08eac360`). It never writes `+0x08` (5), `+0x18` (0) or `+0x20` (1).
- `world_change_time` logged `mode=2` when leaving a map, `mode=6` for End the day and `mode=1` on each map entry.
- Not done: "Advance Time" to a chosen slot. The player did not find it in the PDA.

## C2. NPC pairing: pass, with a correction to the plan

- **The pairing is not redone every day.** `world_change_time` calls `world_assign_daily_spots` (`0x08eab8a4`) only when `0x08eab3b0` returns 1, and that function returns the calendar weekday `+0x1c` (`jal 0x08eab3b0` / `bne v0, 1` at `0x08ea0690`–`0x08ea069c`). End the day from day 0 and day 1 logged `pick_seller` but no `assign_daily_spots` and no write to the pairing order. The order `03 78 34 11 05 41 16 09` (first 8 bytes at `S + 0x1a28c`, `0x08abfa48`) was the same on days 1, 2 and 6. On the change to day 7 (Monday) `assign_daily_spots` ran and the order became `07 05 68 03 42 06 11 09`. So **the pairs are drawn on Mondays and last a week**. `world_pick_sandwich_seller` still runs every day.
- **The first pair stands on neighbouring spots** (read on day 6). The record index is the character id − 2 (record 3 holds character 5, record 120 character 122), so character 3 is record 1 (`0x08abc62c`) and character 120 is record 118 (`0x08abf038`):

  | Char | `+0x00` location | `+0x0c`, `+0x10` cell | `+0x15` dir | `+0x18` spot | `+0x54` flags |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 3 | 21 | 20, 1 | 1 | 334 | `0xa0` |
  | 120 | 21 | 19, 1 | 1 | 335 | `0xa0` |

  Spots 334 and 335 are `(s − 1, s)` with `s` odd. The spot table (`rel_story +0x2092c`) gives `(21, 20, 1, 1)` and `(21, 19, 1, 1)`, the same location, cells and direction as the records. Two other records (characters 5 and 122, read on day 1) matched their spots 100 and 50 the same way. This confirms the record fields `+0x00`, `+0x0c/+0x10`, `+0x15` and `+0x18`.
- On screen, Zane and Nathan stood together at the Slifer Red dorm on days 1–6. On day 7 Zane was at the Obelisk Blue boys' dorm with Alexis.
- **Not as planned: flag 2.** Both members of the pair have flags `0xa0` (flags 6 and 8), but not flag 2 (`0x2`, "placed by the daily pairing"). Either something clears it during the week or the reading of flag 2 is wrong. *Answered later from the code:* `world_assign_daily_spots` clears flag 2 on every paired character and sets it only on character 100 when the count is odd (`world.md`, "Daily schedule").
