## Purpose and flow

Tag-team reversi, scene 0x19 (module id 0x2c). The player and a partner play side 1 against two computer opponents (side 2) on an 8x8 board. Each move is chosen in two halves: on the player's turn the player chooses the row and the partner AI chooses the column; on the opponents' turn one opponent chooses the row and the other the column (or one of them chooses both, more often in later rounds).

- Entry `world_reversi_module_start` registers 0x38 / 0xe4 with block 0x0000f350; frame callback 0x174, leave callback 0x1a0 (not in the packs); `system_scene_return(0x19, 0)` at the end.
- `world_reversi_init` reads `world_get_reversi_params` words 0/1 only (the partner and its variant) and passes -1 for the two opponent seats, so block words 2-5 are never read by this module.
- Module state block 0x0000f360 (0x970 bytes cleared): +0 heap (2 MiB at module +0xfcd0), 0xf364/0xf368 partner, 0xf36c/0xf374 seat 2, 0xf370/0xf378 seat 3, 0xf37c main state, 0xf380 load step, 0xf384 round counter (1..100), 0xf388 total score (passed to the result screen), 0xf38c win counter, 0xf390..0xf3a4 the resolved characters of the round, 0xf3a8/0xf3c8/0xf3e8 their 8-word reversi AI weight tables, 0xf408 flags (bit 0 archive loaded, bit 1 play another round), 0xf40c archive buffer, 0xf410 nested mini_result archive.

Main state machine `world_reversi_update` (0xf37c): 0 load archive, start intro (animation 6, sound 0x7ac6), round 1, scores 0; 1 intro done -> characters (two random opponents, `world_reversi_pick_random_opponent`), board setup, fade in; 2 fade done -> start banner (animation 5); 3 play (`world_reversi_update_play`) until it ends, fade out; 4 either next round (round + 1, new opponents) or the result screen with game index 5 and score {0xf388}; 5 result: 3 leave -> 6, 2 play again -> 0; 6 release the archive and finish.

Play phases `world_reversi_update_play` (0xf480): 1 start banner, 2 row choice, 3 column choice, 4 placement, 5 flip sequence (one disc every 5 frames), 6 turn switch or game end, 7 result banner (win 7 / lose 4 / draw 3, waits for a button at frame 0x14), 8 score panel count-up, 9 continue window (strTbl word 2; Continue sets flag bit 1), 10 done. After a loss the game ends without asking.

## Rules as implemented

- Placement (phase 4): an occupied cell is rejected. Otherwise the bracketed discs in all 8 directions are flipped. A move that flips nothing is still accepted when the cell touches any disc; it is rejected (and the disc removed) only when it has no occupied neighbour. So this reversi does not require captures (confidence high from the code; worth a PPSSPP check).
- No pass rule: the game ends only when the board is full (`world_reversi_has_empty_cell`).
- Starting position: side 1 at (3,3) and (4,4), side 2 at (3,4) and (4,3).
- Win/lose by disc count of side 1 vs side 2.
- Score: chain counter per side (consecutive accepted moves, max 5); for side 1 a chain of 2+ adds (flips + chain * 10) * 10 to the bonus 0xf9ec and plays a chain sound from 0xec98. Round score = (K * wins + 1.0) * (side-1 discs * 50 + bonus), K the float at 0xe7b4; on a win the round score is counted into the total 0xf388 (capped 999999999). The total is what the result screen records (game index 5 -> record 7, DP = score / 50).
- A miss counter per side (consecutive rejected moves) feeds the AI (partner keeps its target after a miss; opponents cooperate after misses).

## AI

- `world_reversi_ai_score_cell(side, row, col, weights)`: -1 for occupied cells or cells without neighbours; with n = discs flipped: corner w[0] + w[1] n; safe edge (not next to a corner, not capturable) w[2] + w[3] n; otherwise w[6] + w[7] n for n < 5 and w[4] + w[5] n for n >= 5; clamped 0..100000. The weights are each character's `world_get_character_words_58` (8 words), so every character has its own reversi personality in the BOOT character table (new finding for `world.md`).
- Best move = best row of per-row bests; random keys break ties. The AI "walks" its cursor along 1-2 stops (a random feint line first) before confirming.
- `world_reversi_ai_opponents_cooperate`: round 1 only after 3 opponent misses, round 2 after 3 misses or 1/5, round 3 after 2 misses or 1/4, round 4 after 1 miss or 1/3, round 5+ always.
- The partner computes the best move on the whole board and only steers the column; it ignores the row the player picked.
- `world_reversi_state_to_side` (0x00003b64) maps the cell state (third argument, a2): 1 and 4 -> side 1, 2 and 3 -> side 2, 0 -> empty.
- `world_reversi_sim_test_line` (0x000044ec): arguments side a0, row a1, col a2, d_row a3, d_col t0, board t1 (12-byte cells, 0x60 per row). It returns 1 when the run of the side's discs on both sides of (row, col) is open at one end and closed by the opponent at the other.
- `world_reversi_sim_apply_flips` does not write the mover's disc on the board copy, so `world_reversi_sim_is_capturable` tests the lines around an empty cell. The result is the same, because `world_reversi_sim_test_line` starts from the neighbouring cells.

## Corrections to existing docs

- `world.md`, "Mini-game parameter blocks": for reversi, words 0/1 are the player's partner (teammate), not the opponent; the two opponents are random (`world_reversi_pick_random_opponent`: characters with a partner deck, special ones only when met). Words 2-5 of the reversi block are not read by rel_reversi.
- `save-minigame-blocks.md` open question "which mini-game passes which game index": rel_quiz passes 3 (record 6, DP / 10) and rel_reversi passes 5 (record 7, DP / 50), both as literal constants at the call of the result-create copy (`world_quiz_result_task_start` and `world_reversi_update` state 4). This also matches `ui_mini_result_is_from_story` mapping game 3 to scene 0x18 (quiz).
